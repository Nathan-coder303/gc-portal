TPO ROOFING SYSTEM
________________________________________________________________________________

[CONTRACTOR] will furnish and install a fully code-compliant TPO Single-Ply Roofing System in accordance with Florida Building Code (FBC 2023), Miami-Dade NOA approvals, and manufacturer specifications (GAF, Mule-Hide, Carlisle, Firestone, or Equal).

1. Removal & Surface Preparation only for existing buildings. For new construction move to step 2.

Remove existing roofing materials down to the structural deck (BUR, modified bitumen, TPO, EPDM, or gravel).
Remove all flashing, fasteners, deteriorated insulation, and wet materials.
Protect surrounding property, HVAC units, and electrical conduits.
Clean deck surface and prepare for substrate inspection.

2. Deck Inspection & Repairs

Inspect wood, metal, or concrete deck for deterioration, rust, cracks, or moisture intrusion.
Replace damaged decking (wood blocking, metal decking, or concrete repairs as required).
Ensure structural substrate meets fastening and uplift requirements.

3. Insulation Installation is not included unless specified here.
(Select One System)

A. Flat ISO Insulation System for 1/8 or more sloped roofs.
Install 1–2 layers of PolyISO insulation mechanically fastened or adhered.
Provide target R-value insulation assembly meeting code or owner preference (R-19 Optional).
Stagger board joints and cover fastener plates before membrane installation.

B. Tapered ISO System for a negative or flat roof surface.
Install tapered ISO insulation package to promote positive drainage.
Achieve minimum 1/8" per foot slope or 1/4" slope toward drains, scuppers, or gutters.
Custom layouts designed per roof area, integrating saddles, crickets, and sumps.
Both systems include perimeter and penetration wood blocking upgrades as required.

4. Cover Board (Optional but Recommended for Commercial Projects)
Install 1/2" high-density polyiso or gypsum cover board (DensDeck or Securock).
Increase impact resistance, uplift performance, and extend membrane lifespan.

5. TPO Membrane Installation
Install 60mil or 80mil TPO membrane, white or gray, mechanically fastened or fully adhered.
Heat-weld all seams using approved hot-air equipment for a continuous watertight bond.
Install reinforced membrane at penetrations, corners, and high-stress areas.
Install walk pads at HVAC access points (commercial requirement).

6. Flashing & Metal Components
Install new TPO-coated metal for edges, parapet caps, scuppers, and perimeter flashing.
Heat-weld membrane to coated metal per manufacturer detail.
Install new pipe boots, pitch pans, curb flashings, and TPO wall flashings.
Re-flash skylights, roof hatches, rooftop units, and penetrations.

7. Drainage, HVAC & Rooftop Equipment
Remove and reinstall drains as needed; install new drain seals and clamping rings.
Seal around RTUs, conduit lines, and mechanical supports using reinforced TPO details.
Clean and verify all roof drains for proper water flow.

8. Final Inspection & Clean Up
Perform thorough welding inspection of all seams and accessories.
Seal all terminations with manufacturer-approved sealant.
Remove all debris, perform magnet sweep, and clean around property.
Prepare for manufacturer inspection (if applicable) and city/county final inspection.

9. Warranty Options
10, 15, or 20-year Manufacturer Warranty depending on membrane thickness & installation method.
[CONTRACTOR] Workmanship Warranty included.

Scope of Work – TREMCO Waterproofing System
________________________________________________________________________________

1. Surface Preparation

* Inspect existing substrate conditions prior to installation
* Remove loose debris, dust, laitance, oils, sealants, and contaminants
* Grind or mechanically prepare surfaces as required for proper adhesion
* Repair cracks, spalls, and surface defects using approved TREMCO repair materials
* Ensure substrate is clean, dry, and structurally sound before waterproofing

2. Crack & Joint Treatment

* Treat all static and dynamic cracks per TREMCO guidelines
* Install joint reinforcement where required
* Apply TREMCO-approved sealant at expansion joints, control joints, and transitions
* Reinforce penetrations, drains, curbs, and vertical-to-horizontal transitions

3. Primer Application

* Apply TREMCO-approved primer compatible with the selected waterproofing system
* Primer to be applied at manufacturer-recommended coverage rates
* Allow proper curing time prior to membrane installation

4. Waterproofing Membrane Installation

* Install TREMCO liquid-applied waterproofing membrane (system type as specified: urethane, PMMA, or modified system)
* Apply membrane at required thickness to achieve full waterproofing coverage
* Embed reinforcing fabric where required (cracks, seams, transitions)
* Ensure continuous, seamless waterproofing with no voids or pinholes

5. Flashings & Detailing

* Waterproof all:
   * Parapet walls
   * Drains and scuppers
   * Pipe penetrations
   * Door thresholds
   * Equipment curbs
* Install membrane up vertical surfaces to manufacturer-required heights
* Seal terminations to ensure watertight integrity

6. Protection & Wear Course (If Applicable)

* Install protection board, drainage mat, or wear coat as specified
* Apply TREMCO topcoat or traffic coating system if area is exposed to foot traffic
* Broadcast aggregate for slip resistance where required

7. Final Inspection & Quality Control

* Inspect completed waterproofing system for continuity and thickness
* Perform manufacturer-recommended checks
* Correct any deficiencies prior to demobilization
* Clean work area and remove all debris from site

8. Warranty

* Installation performed in accordance with TREMCO manufacturer requirements
* Manufacturer warranty available when applicable and approved
* Contractor workmanship warranty provided per contract terms

9. Exclusions (Unless Specifically Included)

* Structural repairs beyond surface preparation
* Concrete replacement
* Tile, pavers, or finished surface installation
* Engineering, testing, or third-party inspections
* Permit fees or city-required upgrades
* Any work not specifically described in this Scope of Work
